What changes in your WhatsApp

When you connect VOS, some WhatsApp Business features stop being available. Most businesses don’t use them, but we’d rather you know beforehand:

  • Broadcast lists: are disabled (existing ones become read-only).
  • Live location: doesn’t work.
  • Editing or deleting sent messages: not available.
  • Profile photo: can’t be changed after connecting (set it the way you want beforehand).
  • Groups and calls: keep working normally in your app, but VOS doesn’t take part.
  • WhatsApp for Windows: not supported.

And one important condition:
You need to open your WhatsApp Business at least once every 13 days. Otherwise, WhatsApp disconnects VOS automatically. It’s WhatsApp’s rule, not ours.
